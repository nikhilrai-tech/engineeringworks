About SB College of Pharmacy

SB College of Pharmacy is a premier institution committed to excellence in pharmaceutical education. Established with a vision to produce skilled and ethical pharmacy professionals, we offer comprehensive programs including Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharma), Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharma), and Polytechnic Diploma courses — all approved by the Pharmacy Council of India and affiliated with the relevant state university.

Our mission is to provide quality education through a student-centered approach, combining rigorous academic coursework with practical laboratory training and industry exposure. With experienced faculty, modern infrastructure, and a supportive learning environment, SB College of Pharmacy is dedicated to empowering students to make meaningful contributions to the pharmaceutical sector and public health.
