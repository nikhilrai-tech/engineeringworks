Our Programs

Academic Programs Tailored for Your Future

Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharma)

A 4-year undergraduate degree approved by the Pharmacy Council of India. Covers pharmaceutics, pharmacology, pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacognosy, and clinical pharmacy — preparing students for careers in the pharmaceutical industry and healthcare sector.

Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharma)

A 2-year diploma program that trains students in the fundamental principles of pharmacy practice. Graduates are eligible to work as licensed pharmacists in retail pharmacies, hospitals, and drug stores across India.

Polytechnic Diploma

Practical, skill-based technical diploma programs in laboratory technology and pharmaceutical manufacturing. These industry-aligned courses are designed to equip students with job-ready technical competencies for immediate employment.

Modern Laboratories

State-of-the-art pharmaceutical, chemistry, microbiology, and instrumentation labs. Students gain practical experience using modern equipment, ensuring they meet industry standards before graduation.

Placement Assistance

Our dedicated placement cell maintains strong ties with leading pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, and research organizations. We conduct campus recruitment drives and provide career counseling to help students secure their ideal roles.

Library & Research Resources

Access an extensive library with thousands of pharmaceutical textbooks, journals, and research publications. Students also benefit from internet facilities and online academic databases to support their studies and research projects.
