About Us

About SB College of Pharmacy

SB College of Pharmacy is a premier institution dedicated to providing quality pharmaceutical education. We offer a wide range of programs including Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharma), Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharma), and Polytechnic Diploma courses — all designed to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to excel in the pharmaceutical and healthcare industries.

Our state-of-the-art laboratories, experienced faculty, and industry-focused curriculum ensure that our graduates are job-ready and well-prepared for professional practice, research, and entrepreneurship in the field of pharmacy. We are committed to academic excellence, innovation, and the holistic development of every student.

Our Programs

Academic Programs Tailored for Your Future

Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharma)

A 4-year undergraduate degree program that provides in-depth knowledge of pharmaceutical sciences, drug development, pharmacology, and clinical practice. Recognized by the Pharmacy Council of India.

Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharma)

A 2-year diploma program that prepares students for careers as pharmacy technicians, retail pharmacists, and healthcare assistants. An ideal entry point into the pharmaceutical field.

Polytechnic Diploma

Practical technical diploma programs designed to build hands-on skills in laboratory technology, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and allied technical fields for immediate employment.

Modern Laboratories

Our college features fully equipped pharmaceutical, chemistry, and microbiology labs that provide students with hands-on experience using modern instruments and industry-standard equipment.

Placement Assistance

Our dedicated placement cell connects graduates with leading pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, and research organizations, ensuring a smooth transition from academic life to professional careers.

Student Support Services

We offer comprehensive student support including academic counseling, mentorship programs, career guidance, and extracurricular activities to ensure the holistic development of every student.
